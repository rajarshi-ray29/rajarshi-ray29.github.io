The Problem

Your best content is locked in videos nobody can search.

Course creators and instructors sit on hundreds of hours of recorded gold: cohort replays, lectures, Q&A sessions. But learners can't find anything in it, so the same questions arrive again and again.

🔁

The same 50 questions, every cohort

Hours of founder and TA time every week go to answering questions whose answers already exist on video. FAQ docs and pinned posts don't get read, and support cost grows linearly with enrollment.

🗄️

A video library nobody opens

Students won't scrub a 90-minute replay to find one answer. Past ~20 videos, chapter markers and search-by-title collapse, and your biggest asset quietly generates zero ongoing value.

📉

Stuck learners silently churn

The student who can't find an answer at 11pm doesn't post in the community; they disengage. Confusion shows up as refunds, low completion, and bad exam distributions, long after it started.

How It Works

Live in an afternoon. Not a semester.

01

Connect your library

Point SceneSenseAI at a Google Drive folder or a YouTube playlist. Your videos stay where they are (only transcripts and a search index are kept), and new uploads are picked up automatically.

02

Ask, then jump to the moment

Every question gets a grounded answer with clickable timestamp citations. Click one and the built-in player opens the exact second of the exact video: your frameworks, your words, verifiable instantly.

03

Learn with a team of agents

Beyond Q&A, three teaching agents work over your whole library: a quiz master that scores you and targets weak spots, a tutor that teaches step by step, and a navigator that maps it all as a knowledge graph.

Why It's Different

Built for trust, not just answers.

Generic chatbots hallucinate in front of your paying students. SceneSenseAI is designed around one principle: every answer must be traceable to your content.

⏱️

Timestamp-cited answers

Every response cites minute-and-second moments in your own videos, and clicking a citation plays that exact second in the built-in player. A confident wrong answer never costs you your brand, because the source is one click away.

🧑‍🏫

A teaching team, not one chatbot

A quiz master builds multiple-choice quizzes from your videos and remembers weak spots. A tutor explains, then asks one small question back. A navigator turns the whole library into a searchable knowledge graph.

🔒

Your content stays yours

Videos never leave your Drive or your channel: SceneSenseAI keeps only transcripts and a search index, and never trains shared models on your curriculum. Every answer carries a plain-language "AI-generated, click to verify" notice.

📊

Instructor analytics (in the works)

Next up, being shaped with founding pilots: share a topic with one link (no student accounts needed) and see every question, every AI answer, and where learners get stuck, before it shows up in refunds or exams.
