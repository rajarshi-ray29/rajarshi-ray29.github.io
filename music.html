The Journey

Quiet reps, loud payoff.

Five years ago I started posting singing covers to an audience of nobody. This month, that same voice was live on international radio.

A while back I picked one long-term project: record a song in every Indian language I could learn to sing in. Bengali. Tamil. Hindi. English. No deadline, no audience — just showing up.

My singing teacher taught me something I didn't expect from a music lesson: technique gets you in tune. Understanding the weight a song carries is what actually moves someone listening.

Then someone who'd found those uploads invited me onto her radio show. I sang live in Hindi, co-hosted parts of it in English, and heard from listeners tuning in from different corners of the world — in real time. No do-overs, no editing, just whatever came out.

My sister recorded the whole thing from the side of the room on her phone. That's the version I'm keeping forever.
