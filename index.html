01 · About

Engineer by trade, researcher at heart, entrepreneur by obsession.

I'm Rajarshi: born in Virginia, raised in Bangalore (India's tech capital), and now building AI in New York. That mix of places gave me a simple operating principle: great technology should feel effortless to the person using it, no matter how hard it was to build.

After computer science at BITS Pilani, I finished my M.S. in Computer Science at Stony Brook University, researching generative vision (diffusion models, VAEs, and image restoration) under Prof. Klaus Mueller. That work took me to CVPR 2025, where my team ranked 18th globally in the NTIRE Super-Resolution challenge.

Today I'm a Staff-level AI Engineer at IBM, architecting a patent-pending agentic AI system for the z17 LinuxONE platform and collaborating with IBM Research on agentic workflows for industrial robotics. On nights and weekends, I'm a part-time entrepreneur running SceneSenseAI, building AI teaching assistants that make video course libraries actually searchable.

The through-line: I want to keep doing this at the deepest level possible, which is why the next stop is a PhD.
